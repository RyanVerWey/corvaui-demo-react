# Design Direction: Asterline

## Creative North Star

**The living network.** Asterline should feel as consequential, interconnected, and precise as the infrastructure it helps operate. Public pages combine large documentary images, restrained editorial typography, ruled information, and deliberate negative space. The command center shifts into a dense operational mode without changing the semantic system.

## CorvaUI Contract

CorvaUI components, tokens, themes, and interaction states remain authoritative. Application CSS controls page composition, responsive arrangement, image cropping, and entrance motion. It must not introduce app-level color tokens, recreate controls, or recolor package components.

## Color and Themes

Concept Light and Concept Dark are used unchanged. Neutral canvases carry the page so accent and semantic colors retain meaning. Broad teal or blue washes are avoided. Photography supplies material color and atmosphere.

## Typography

Use the published CorvaUI typeface and fixed breakpoint scales. Marketing display type is reserved for true page statements. Operational headings remain compact. Letter spacing is zero, and brand or critical words must not split awkwardly.

## Composition

- Hero imagery is full bleed with text over the actual scene, never in a decorative card.
- Editorial sections alternate open layouts, full-width media, and ruled information.
- Repeated cards are limited to individual articles or case-study items.
- Cards are never nested.
- Dense product panels use stable grids and component-owned overflow.
- The command center prioritizes scanability and repeated action over marketing scale.

## Motion

Entrance motion is restrained and purposeful. Image and article hover movement is subtle. CorvaUI owns control hover, focus, pressed, and chart animation behavior. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential animation.

## Image Set

The local Asterline image set covers an intermodal port, offshore wind inspection, a transit control room, and connected urban infrastructure. Assets are generated specifically for this demonstration and contain no third-party logos or placeholder treatments.

## Restrictions

Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, generic SaaS composition, excessive pills, one-note colored backgrounds, custom controls, stock-photo posing, fake holographic interfaces, and copy that turns the public experience into a design-system catalog.
